# Rimota Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Rimota ("the app") is a remote control, screen-mirror and file tool for devices you own. **We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data on any server.** SAMZLAB operates no backend for the app and the app contains no ads and no analytics.

## What stays on your phone
- Names and IP addresses of the TVs and PCs you add, saved commands, command history, an ADB key pair used to pair with your TV, and PC pairing passwords (encrypted with the Android Keystore).
- A local diagnostic log (Tools, Logs). It leaves your phone only if you choose "Report a problem", "Share" or "Copy all" and send it yourself. It contains no passwords.
This data is excluded from Android backups and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Network use
- The app connects directly to devices you choose on your own network (ADB, screen mirror and PC remote). Screen content from your TV or PC goes straight to your phone and nowhere else.
- Optional "Anywhere" access uses Tailscale (a separate app by Tailscale Inc., under its own privacy policy) or a Cloudflare quick tunnel started by the Windows helper at your request.
- To find missing app icons the app may look up the public Google Play page of an app installed on your TV.

## Google Play services
Purchases (Rimota Pro) are processed by Google Play Billing; we only learn whether a purchase is valid. The app also uses Google Play to check that it was installed from Google Play and to offer updates. Google's privacy policy applies to those services.

## Permissions
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, used to reach and discover devices on your network. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera or storage permissions.

## Children
Rimota is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version and date will be published on this page.
