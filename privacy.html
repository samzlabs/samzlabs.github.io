Rimota — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Contact: samzlabpk@gmail.com

Rimota ("the app") is a remote control, screen-mirror and file tool for devices you own. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data on any server. The developer operates no backend for the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Names and IP addresses of TVs/PCs you add, saved commands, command history, an ADB key pair used to pair with your TV, and PC pairing passwords (encrypted with the Android Keystore).
  • A local diagnostic log (Tools → Logs). It never leaves your phone unless you press "Copy all" and share it yourself.

This data is excluded from Android backups and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Network use

  • The app connects directly to devices you choose on your local network (ADB, screen-mirror and PC-remote connections). Screen content from your TV/PC goes straight to your phone.
  • Optional "Anywhere" access uses Tailscale (a separate app by Tailscale Inc., its own privacy policy applies) or a Cloudflare quick-tunnel started by the Windows helper at your request.

Purchases

Rimota Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes payment; we receive only whether the purchase is valid. See Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — to reach and discover devices on your network. No location, contacts, microphone, camera or storage permission is requested.

Children

Rimota is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be published at this address. Questions: samzlabpk@gmail.com.
